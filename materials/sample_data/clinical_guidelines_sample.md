# Clinical Guidelines for Emergency Medicine & Endocrinology

## Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA) Management
Diabetic ketoacidosis is an acute, major life-threatening complication of diabetes mellitus characterized by hyperglycemia, ketoacidosis, and ketonuria.
First-line therapy consists of prompt intravenous fluid resuscitation with normal saline (0.9% NaCl) at 15-20 mL/kg/hour during the first hour.
Regular insulin infusion should be initiated at 0.1 units/kg/hour after ensuring potassium levels are greater than 3.3 mEq/L.
Potassium repletion is vital: if serum potassium is between 3.3 and 5.2 mEq/L, administer 20-30 mEq K+ per liter of IV fluid.
Bicarbonate therapy is generally contraindicated unless severe arterial acidosis (pH < 6.9) is present.

## Acute Myocardial Infarction (STEMI)
Primary percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI) is the preferred reperfusion strategy when performed within 90 minutes of medical contact.
Immediate pharmacotherapy includes chewable aspirin (162 to 325 mg), unfractionated heparin, and a P2Y12 platelet inhibitor (Ticagrelor 180 mg or Prasugrel 60 mg).
Beta-blockers should be initiated within the first 24 hours in hemodynamically stable patients without signs of acute heart failure or cardiogenic shock.
Contraindications to beta-blockers include PR interval > 0.24 seconds, second- or third-degree heart block, active bronchospasm, and cardiogenic shock.

## Acute Sepsis & Septic Shock Guidelines
Sepsis is defined as life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host response to infection.
The Hour-1 bundle mandates:
1. Measure blood lactate level. Remeasure if initial lactate is elevated (> 2 mmol/L).
2. Obtain blood cultures prior to administration of antibiotics.
3. Administer broad-spectrum intravenous antimicrobials.
4. Rapidly administer 30 mL/kg crystalloids for hypotension or lactate ≥ 4 mmol/L.
5. Apply vasopressors if hypotensive during or after fluid resuscitation to maintain mean arterial pressure (MAP) ≥ 65 mm Hg. First-choice vasopressor is norepinephrine.
